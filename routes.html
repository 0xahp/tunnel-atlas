What to run in 2026, and the path it takes

With no censorship, run WireGuard. In a censored country, start with VLESS + REALITY on your own domain and keep Hysteria2 as a backup. In a shutdown, use a relay inside the country.

On this page

No censorship: WireGuard

WireGuard had the lowest battery cost of five VPNs in the only independent phone test, and it stays silent when idle. Nothing hides it, which only matters if someone is looking.

Censored country: REALITY on your own domain

VLESS + REALITY + Vision was reported working in Iran and Russia in 2026. Using your own domain means the name, DNS and IP all agree, so a name-against-IP check finds nothing. Keep Hysteria2 as a UDP backup, and a CDN route for when your server's IP gets blocked.

Shutdown or allowlist: a relay inside the country

During allowlists the protocol matters less than the IP address. A relay in a domestic data-centre that keeps foreign access stays reachable when international links are cut. DNS tunnels are very slow, but they kept working on some ISPs in Iran's Feb–May 2026 blackout.

What we don't know

Nobody has measured phone battery for proxy protocols, so the efficiency ranking comes from tests of VPN protocols. Shutdowns change week to week, and net4people has had no new field reports on what works in Iran since early August 2026 (as of 17 Oct). In Turkmenistan any foreign IP gets banned after moderate traffic, whatever the protocol.
