  1. ECH (RFC 2026)

    Disguise layer · Cutting edge. Encrypts the website name in the TLS hello.

    How
    An outer hello shows a shared name (such as cloudflare-ech.com); the real name is encrypted inside. Keys come from DNS.
    Hides
    Which site you're visiting. Russia blocks Cloudflare's ECH (outer name cloudflare-ech.com) since Nov 2024; ECH with other outer names passed.
    Runs over
    TLS
    Found in
    Xray, sing-box, mihomo
    Status
    Standardised as RFC 9849 in Mar 2026.
    Censorship, 2026
    Iran: No data; China: No data; Russia: Degraded; Turkmenistan: No data. Russia blocks Cloudflare's ECH (cloudflare-ech.com) since Nov 2024; other outer names passed (n4p #417). No other 2026 reports.
    Connection
    TCP
    Power on a phone (estimate)
    Moderate

  13. AmneziaWG (2023 · v2 2025 · v3.1 2026)

    Classic VPN · Cutting edge. WireGuard with disguise: junk packets and random sizes so the handshake doesn't match.

    How
    Same crypto as WireGuard. Adds junk packets before the handshake (Jc), random padding (S1–S4), changeable headers (H1–H4), and packets that mimic DNS, QUIC or SIP (I1–I5). 3.1 adds header protection.
    Hides
    By breaking WireGuard's fixed signature. Still random-looking UDP, which some networks throttle.
    Runs over
    UDP
    Found in
    mihomo (WireGuard amnezia options), AmneziaVPN; not in upstream Xray/sing-box
    Status
    Active; built for Russia's blocks (2023 on). Reported working in Iran (Jun 2026). In Russia, even AmneziaWG-only server IPs were caught in mass IP bans (Aug–Sep 2026). One Iranian blog advises staying under ~300 GB a month, and a 2025 study found MCI blocks proxy IPs in proportion to their connection count (refraction proxies, 2023–25 data).
    Censorship, 2026
    Iran: Works (Reported working, Jun 2026 (n4p #628)); China: No data; Russia: Works (Works, but servers caught in mass IP bans, Aug–Sep 2026 (n4p #671)); Turkmenistan: Degraded (Banned after moderate traffic (one user, Aug 2026); a low-traffic server lasted 2 years (2025)).
    Connection
    UDP
    Power on a phone (estimate)
    Low

  15. Hysteria2 (2023)

    QUIC / UDP · Popular. A QUIC proxy built for bad, lossy links. Looks like an HTTP/3 website.

    How
    QUIC handshake, then an HTTP/3 login (success = status 233). Its Brutal congestion control keeps sending at a set rate when packets are lost, where TCP would slow down.
    Hides
    As HTTP/3, or as random UDP with Salamander. With Salamander on, probes can't reach the masquerade site at all, so it's Salamander (and a clean IP) doing the work. Dies where UDP or QUIC is blocked. A 2025 study found its aggressive congestion control can itself give it away.
    Runs over
    QUIC (UDP)
    Found in
    Xray, sing-box, mihomo, v2fly; 29 of 38 apps
    Status
    Active. Reported working in Iran except on Rightel (one user, Jun 2026).
    Censorship, 2026
    Iran: Works (All ISPs except Rightel (one user), 13 Jun 2026; fails in blackouts and where UDP is dropped); China: Degraded (Residential UDP QoS cuts long sessions); Russia: Degraded (UDP blocked on mobile); Turkmenistan: Blocked (Banned with and without Salamander and port hopping).

  16. REALITY (2023)

    Disguise layer · Popular. Borrows a real website's TLS handshake. No domain or certificate needed.

    How
    The client hides its login inside the TLS Session ID, sealed with the server's public key. Clients that fail it, including censors' probes, are passed straight to the real website and see its genuine certificate.
    Hides
    As a visit to the borrowed site. Weak spot: a famous site's name on a small VPS address. 'Self-steal' uses your own domain to fix that.
    Runs over
    TCP + TLS 1.3
    Found in
    Xray, sing-box, mihomo; named in 16 apps
    Status
    Best-reported TCP option in 2026.
    Censorship, 2026
    Iran: Works (Working again from mid-Jun 2026; needs IP rotation. Borrowed names throttled, self-steal fine (field test)); China: Works (Replay-probe risk on some targets (Xray #6121)); Russia: Works (Self-steal reported working (one user, Oct 2026); popular VPS providers' IPs mass-banned); Turkmenistan: Blocked (IPs banned after moderate traffic (n4p #523)).
    Connection
    TCP
    Power on a phone (estimate)
    Moderate
