Up to eight Mac apps sent the same traffic through the same servers, one app at a time, on battery. Three setups, measured separately: pick one to see its leaderboard and every measure behind it.

Score = 35% efficiency under traffic + 35% efficiency at idle + 30% performance; lower is better. Numbers are medians over all rounds; app power and energy are macOS's own per-process estimates for the tunnel process.
