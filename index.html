How we tested: One MacBook Air M1 (macOS 27.0.1), on battery, screen off, no app windows open. Each app ran in its TUN (whole-system VPN) mode with the same Shadowsocks 2022 profile (2022-blake3-aes-128-gcm), pointed at a sing-box server on the same Mac; iperf3 3.22 sent traffic through the app's tunnel to that server and back. No internet and no remote server are involved, so the numbers show each app's own overhead. A script switched the apps on and off one at a time, in two rounds (the second in reverse order); each number is the median of the two. Speed: 5 s per test after a 2 s warm-up, over one and over four parallel connections. Efficiency: a steady 200 Mbit/s download for 20 s; energy is macOS's own estimate for the app's tunnel process, CPU is that process's CPU time, both per GB moved, and memory is its footprint at the end of the test. Every test checked that its bytes went through the app's tunnel; a test with any keyboard or trackpad input was thrown out and repeated. MTU is the tunnel's packet size as macOS reported it: we asked for 4000 where we could; apps that stay at 1500 handle about 2.7× more packets per GB (Happ's MTU setting has no effect on the Mac tunnel). Karing used its mixed stack; its four-connection download is from one round. Versions as installed on 20 Oct 2026. The sing-box command line (no app, no GUI) is ranked with the apps: it shows what the core alone costs. Score (out of 100) = 30% speed + 70% efficiency, each as a ratio to the best app: 100 × (average speed ÷ best average speed)^0.3 × (best energy per GB ÷ energy per GB)^0.7. Efficiency weighs more because every app near the top is already far faster than any real internet connection. Click a column header to sort by that column instead.

iPhone check (16 Oct): on an iPhone 11 Pro (iOS 27, same chain + rules, 2 rounds, whole-phone power from the battery gauge over USB), Anywhere 2.4 and Egern tied: 0.93 W vs 0.93–0.95 W at a steady 50 Mbit/s download (about 148–152 J per GB for the whole phone), the same single-connection speed (70–75 Mbit/s) and page first-byte times within run-to-run noise. Idle draw was within noise of no VPN for both. The Mac's ~4× gap in app energy per GB is in the app's own small share of power and doesn't show at the whole-device level.

Footnote: this is not a professional lab test, just a personal, nerdy-interest one: one Mac on Wi-Fi, a few days, a handful of rounds. On a phone over cellular the results may differ.
