Shadowrocket: speed and battery benchmark

Shadowrocket ranked 12th of 24 apps in our Mac lab and 3rd of 7 apps in our iPhone lab, by a score that weighs speed 30% and energy efficiency 70%. Every app was tested the same way, one at a time, with a test server on the device itself.

Mac lab: 12th of 24

Shadowrocket's best result here was memory at 200 Mbit/s (2nd of 24) and its weakest was average speed (15th of 24). It used 2.0× the energy per GB of Egern, the lightest app here, and reached 12% of the speed of Throne, the fastest.
