Egern: speed and battery benchmark

Egern ranked 4th of 24 apps in our Mac lab and 1st of 7 apps in our iPhone lab, by a score that weighs speed 30% and energy efficiency 70%. Every app was tested the same way, one at a time, with a test server on the device itself.

Mac lab: 4th of 24

Egern's best result here was energy at 200 Mbit/s (1st of 24) and its weakest was upload, 4 connections (13th of 24). It was the lightest app in this lab, and reached 23% of the speed of Throne, the fastest.
