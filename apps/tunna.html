Tunna: speed and battery benchmark

Tunna ranked 18th of 24 apps in our Mac lab, by a score that weighs speed 30% and energy efficiency 70%. Every app was tested the same way, one at a time, with a test server on the device itself.

Mac lab: 18th of 24

Each app tunnels traffic to a test server on the same Mac, so no network is in the way and the numbers show the app's own cost. Ranked by speed (30%) and energy efficiency (70%).
