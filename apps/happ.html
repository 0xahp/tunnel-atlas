Happ: speed and battery benchmark

Happ ranked 24th of 24 apps in our Mac lab and 7th of 7 apps in our iPhone lab, by a score that weighs speed 30% and energy efficiency 70%. Every app was tested the same way, one at a time, with a test server on the device itself.

Mac lab: 24th of 24

Happ's best result here was memory at 200 Mbit/s (8th of 24) and its weakest was average speed (24th of 24). It used 55× the energy per GB of Egern, the lightest app here, and reached 2% of the speed of Throne, the fastest. Among the 7 apps built on Xray, it ranks 7th.
