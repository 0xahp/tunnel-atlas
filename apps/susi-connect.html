susi connect: speed and battery benchmark

susi connect ranked 19th of 24 apps in our Mac lab, by a score that weighs speed 30% and energy efficiency 70%. Every app was tested the same way, one at a time, with a test server on the device itself.

Mac lab: 19th of 24

susi connect's best result here was memory at 200 Mbit/s (12th of 24) and its weakest was upload, 1 connection (20th of 24). It used 25× the energy per GB of Egern, the lightest app here, and reached 4% of the speed of Throne, the fastest. Among the 7 apps built on Xray, it ranks 4th.
