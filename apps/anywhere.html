Anywhere: speed and battery benchmark

Anywhere ranked 11th of 24 apps in our Mac lab and 2nd of 7 apps in our iPhone lab, by a score that weighs speed 30% and energy efficiency 70%. Every app was tested the same way, one at a time, with a test server on the device itself.

Mac lab: 11th of 24

Anywhere's best result here was memory at 200 Mbit/s (4th of 24) and its weakest was energy at 200 Mbit/s (13th of 24). It used 2.8× the energy per GB of Egern, the lightest app here, and reached 38% of the speed of Throne, the fastest.
